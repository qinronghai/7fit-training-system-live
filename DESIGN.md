# Violet Bangle — "a beaded hoop on a white sheet, under a violet-to-fuchsia sky"
**Pick it when** the product sorts one running total into a few named buckets and should feel upbeat, not stern: usage diaries, allowances, meters, quotas, household logs, anything read month by month.

- **Ground.** Light-led. Every screen opens under a saturated band: violet `#8A3CE6` → orchid `#A93BF2` → fuchsia `#D650DC`, top-left to bottom-right, with two huge crisp tonal discs (white 6–7 %). Below, a lilac-grey page `#F5F4F9`; white sheets r16 overlap the band by 70–110 pt. Wells and amount pills `#F4F3F8`. A section may re-dye the band in its own hue, e.g. deep teal `#07806F` → leaf `#3DBB86`. Dark: page `#14111C`, sheets `#1E1A29`, wells `#2A2538`; the band is unchanged.
- **Text.** Ink `#1D1B26` / `#6B6877`, hairlines `#EEEDF3`; white on the band. Dark: `#F4F1FA` / `#A7A2B5`, hairlines `#2E293B`.
- **Colour.** Six bead hues, one per bucket on every screen: violet `#9A4DF0`, teal `#3FCFB6`, sky `#7FA8EC`, amber `#EEB14B`, pink `#E88FC3`, coral `#ED7A78`; bars may add rose `#FF93B8` and lilac `#BD8CF5`. Colour names the bucket, never good or bad. Black `#0B0812` is kept for the seal.
- **Type.** One heavy geometric grotesk (Avenir Next stands in). Lead title Heavy 58 white, set tight; section heads Bold 23; hero figure Heavy 52 with a 16 pt unit; rows Medium 16.5; pill figures DemiBold 15; band tabs Bold 14 capitals; labels 10.5–12.5 capitals, tracking +17 %. Chinese: PingFang SC Heavy titles, Medium rows; figures keep the grotesk.
- **Shape & depth.** Band tabs are bare white capitals, the active one in a white 18 % capsule; a centred period line sits between thin chevrons. Rows carry a 28 pt solid bead-coloured circle with a white glyph, the name, and an r8 grey pill for the amount; each group ends in an add row. Bars are chubby and round-topped, 44–58 pt wide, on dashed gridlines, with a same-colour glow; figure and caps label beneath. Sheets cast one wide violet-tinted shadow. The lead screen may carry a black 24-tooth scalloped seal on a lighter violet disc, with white laurel wreaths around two lesser figures.
- **Emphasis device.** (1) The bangle: an 18 pt hoop cut into round-capped beads in true proportion, the leading bucket one long violet arc; a violet disc glyph, the heavy figure and a caps label sit inside. (2) The black seal: one figure per product, on the lead screen only.
- **Never.** Blue-to-purple or rainbow gradients; gradients inside beads, bars or icons; square icon tiles; outline icons in rows; one hue shared by two buckets; red and green as verdicts; the seal used for ratings, awards or a second number; dark text on the band; thin type weights.
